Phase 2 — Ongoing Intelligence
Quarterly Diagnostic Architecture
Four intelligence layers run in sequence each quarter. Each produces its own report. All four feed directly into the Impact Report. This is the continuous engine that keeps strategy current and the client informed by real buyer behavior, not last quarter's assumptions.
Layer 1 — Who's Engaging

Audience Segment Intelligence

Analyzes which personas, seniority levels, industries, company sizes, and geographies are actually engaging. Maps real buyer behavior back to ICP assumptions. Discovers personas the client didn't know they had. Identifies seniority drift, off-ICP leakage, and waste at the persona level.

Interactive HTML Report Sources: DemandSense Rate data, LinkedIn exports, campaign CSVs
Layer 2 — Which Accounts Are Moving

Account Intelligence + Pipeline

Named-account funnel: Reached, Clicked, Site Visit, Prospect ID'd, Pipeline, Customer. Scored hot-account leaderboard with multi-signal profiles. Cross-channel signals by named account. Surfaces accounts with pipeline at zero ad exposure and high-engagement accounts not yet in CRM.

Interactive HTML Report Sources: Rate Accounts CSV, DemandSense journeys, CRM data, WebID
Layer 3 — Where Money Goes

Marketing Ecosystem Efficiency

Full marketing system mapped by funnel stage. Channel efficiency by role, warm audience saturation, budget allocation analysis, day-parting heatmaps, pipeline attribution by channel mix, and expansion readiness assessment with phased budget recommendation.

Interactive HTML Report Sources: Campaign exports, day-part CSVs, Optmyzr, Windsor.ai
Layer 4 — What Message Works

Messaging + Creative Performance

Which messaging angles, ad copy, headlines, and content themes drive engagement and conversions. Persona-to-messaging map. Format performance by funnel stage. Search term analysis reveals how prospects describe their own pain. White space for what to test next.

Interactive HTML Report Sources: Creative exports, search terms, Search Console, ad copy analysis
